AIChatExporter

An Android app that turns AI conversations (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek) into documents: Word, PowerPoint, PDF, HTML, Markdown, plain text and JSON. Conversations stay on the phone by default; there is no account on our servers and no tracking.

Early stage, not ready for general use. Use it only on content you have the right to access; any unauthorised or unlawful use is the user's own responsibility.
